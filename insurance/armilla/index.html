Agent Risk / Insurance

Insurance · Lloyd's coverholder / MGA

Armilla

Affirmative AI liability with dedicated AI aggregate limits of $25M or more per organisation, covering AI model underperformance and AI agent mistakes. Liability backed by Chaucer, Axis and Convex; the performance warranty by Chaucer, Greenlight Re and Swiss Re. Paired with Armilla's own AI assessment.
